# AI-elements audit

Audit of the first build (up to commit `0d74b7f`) for anything that was machine-generated or
that reads as a generic "AI website". Every finding below was replaced with real, openly licensed
material or plain human design. Sources and licences for everything brought in are in
[CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).

## Findings

| # | Area | What was there | Why it was a problem | Replaced with |
|---|------|----------------|----------------------|---------------|
| 1 | Photography | 21 images generated by `scripts/make-placeholders.mjs` (SVG gradients + noise, "PLACEHOLDER" labels) | Procedurally generated, not photographs | Real photographs from public-domain / Unsplash / NASA collections published in open-source repos (see CREDITS.md). Generator script deleted |
| 2 | Book covers | Generated "Life Lighter · SAMPLE COVER" images | Fake covers of real books | Typographic title cards built in HTML/CSS, clearly not covers, until the ministry supplies the real cover files |
| 3 | Social image | `og-default.jpg` cropped from a generated gradient | Generated | Cropped from a real photograph |
| 4 | Background films | HyperFrames loops of generated gradients with a procedural "gold dust" particle field and synthetic light-leak blobs | Generated imagery plus two stock "AI cinematic" effects | Re-rendered from the real photographs using only camera moves and cross-dissolves. Particles and leaks removed from the template |
| 5 | Fonts | Inter + Cormorant Garamond (Fontsource) | The default pairing of AI site generators | Archivo (Omnibus-Type) for headlines and Source Serif 4 (Adobe) for text, downloaded from the foundries' own GitHub repositories, subset to Latin, self-hosted with their OFL licences |
| 6 | Emblem | Hand-plotted SVG paths (circle → flame → cross) inside a rotating "INSPIRING PEOPLE · SHARING LIFE" text ring | Invented artwork; rotating text badges are a common generated-site motif | Morph between Phosphor icon shapes (sun → fire → cross, MIT licence). Text ring removed |
| 7 | Icons | Hand-written arrow, play and YouTube SVG paths | Approximated from memory | Phosphor icons (MIT) |
| 8 | Texture | SVG `feTurbulence` film-grain overlay animated on every dark section | Procedural noise, a stock "premium" effect | Removed |
| 9 | Palette | Navy `#0d1220` + gold `#c8a15a` | The default "luxury" palette of generated sites | Paper, ink and a red taken from red-letter Bible print. Still provisional until the ministry's brand colours are confirmed |
| 10 | Headline formula | Every heading: serif line + second line in gold italic ("A word in *due season*") | The most recognisable AI landing-page pattern | Single-voice condensed headlines, no decorative second line |
| 11 | Ornaments | Eyebrow labels with a leading rule, ✦ sparkle separators, outlined giant footer text, magnetic/elastic buttons | Template tropes | Plain labels, a scripture ribbon with ordinary punctuation, solid footer type, normal buttons |
| 12 | Copy | "Gatherings that change everything", "Minds renewed. Lives transformed.", "A word for every season", lists in threes, "transform/inspire millions" phrasing | Reads as generated (rule of three, significance inflation, slogan headlines) | Rewritten in plain language using only verifiable facts. Remaining provisional copy is marked `TODO` for the content audit against the current site |
| 13 | Sample content | Invented sermon titles ("Faith that speaks" …) and events | Invented content | Kept only as clearly labelled samples (`placeholder: true` + visible tag) with neutral titles, to be deleted before launch |

## What was kept

- The real tagline, "Inspiring People. Sharing Life.", from the current site.
- Real data: the YouTube sermon *The Ministry of Giving III*, the four *Life Lighter* editions and their Kindle links, the contact email and social accounts.
- The motion system (GSAP, View Transitions). It's code, not generated media, and it now animates real assets.

## Still outstanding

The current site, emmanuelmakandiwa.com, is blocked by this environment's network policy. Real photos of
Emmanuel and Ruth Makandiwa, the logo, brand colours and the original copy all have to come from there
(or from the ministry). Until then, portrait slots use non-portrait photographs and say so in their alt text.
